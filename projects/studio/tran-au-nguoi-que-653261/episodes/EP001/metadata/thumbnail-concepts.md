# Thumbnail concepts — EP001

The export is vertical (9:16, 30 seconds), so concepts are composed for a vertical Short thumbnail.
The uploaded image should preserve the two visible character designs and accurately represent the
video. No image has been generated; these are concept and text drafts.

## Short text options

1. **ĐỎ VS XANH** — identifies the matchup at a glance.
2. **AI NÉ ĐÒN?** — a question about the visible evasion action, not the winner.
3. **NĂNG LƯỢNG** — highlights the visible energy effects.
4. **DỪNG TAY!** — creates contrast with the friendly ending; use only with a frame that still shows
   both fighters clearly.
5. **TÔN TRỌNG NHAU** — supports the bowing ending.

## Three title + thumbnail pairings

| Title | Thumbnail text | Why they work together |
|---|---|---|
| Hai người que giao đấu mãn nhãn, rồi cúi chào nhau | **ĐỎ VS XANH** | The title promises the action and payoff; the image text names the visual matchup. |
| Người Que Đai Đỏ vs Đai Xanh: Trận Đấu Khép Lại Bằng Cúi Chào | **NĂNG LƯỢNG** | The title gives the matchup and ending; the thumbnail emphasizes a visible action beat. |
| Người que 2D: né đòn, phản công, tung năng lượng | **TÔN TRỌNG NHAU** | The title lists the action; the thumbnail adds the friendly resolution. |

Use a clean frame/composition with both figures visible, their belt colors unmistakable, and the
red/blue effects kept away from faces and silhouettes. Avoid adding a winner, wounds, extra fighters,
spectators, weapons, franchise marks, or claims not shown in the clip. Keep text large and short.
