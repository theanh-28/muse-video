# THEANHREVIEW — YouTube channel profile

Use this as a starting voice and audience profile when preparing YouTube metadata for this channel.
The final video and the user's current instructions remain the source of truth for each upload.

## Audience and language

- Primary language: Vietnamese.
- Audience: Vietnamese-speaking viewers interested in anime, manhua, animation, and Chinese story worlds.
- The channel covers different genres over time, including fantasy, cultivation, martial arts, action,
  comedy, survival, apocalypse, and animated storytelling. Do not assume that every episode belongs
  to one of these genres; classify each video from its actual content.

## Voice

- Write naturally in Vietnamese, with clear, conversational phrasing.
- Make titles specific to the episode's central matchup, dilemma, unusual event, or payoff.
- Use curiosity when the video supports it. Do not promise a reveal, victory, ranking, percentage,
  official status, source title, or plot event that the video does not establish.
- Avoid repeated all-purpose clickbait, keyword piles, excessive capitalization, and filler phrases.
- Let the title explain the premise and let thumbnail text add a short visual hook; do not repeat the
  exact same wording in both.
- Use short Vietnamese thumbnail text that remains readable on a phone and matches visible imagery.
- Keep descriptions informative and specific to the actual video. Do not invent episode credits,
  links, social accounts, release schedules, or calls to action.
- Use only tags and hashtags that accurately describe the video. Tags are metadata, not a promise of
  reach.

## Editorial boundaries

- Treat supplied scripts, bibles, and storyboards as supporting context; inspect the finished video
  before making claims about what viewers will see.
- If audio or subtitles are unavailable or unclear, do not invent dialogue, narration, lyrics, or
  sound-source details.
- Do not claim an official anime/manhua adaptation, franchise, character name, studio, or creator
  unless a reliable source supplied for that episode confirms it.
- Preserve ambiguity when the video does not clearly establish who wins, what a character intends,
  or what happens off-screen.
- This profile is for metadata voice and audience fit. It does not authorize account access,
  publishing, scheduling, or paid promotion.
